Inner loop. P and I act on rate error. D acts on the measured rate only, so stick moves don't produce a D spike. The sum u is a fraction of available motor authority.

Bode · open loop L(jω)

Solid: open-loop gain and phase. Dashed: closed-loop |T|. Phase margin is read at the 0 dB crossover; gain margin at −180°.

Nyquist

Radius compressed beyond 1 (rings at 1, 10, 100). The loop is stable when the curve keeps the −1 point on its left. Closest approach to −1 is 1/Ms.

Step response (linear)

Unit step in the setpoint through the linearised model, including delay and motor lag. Band is ±2%.

How to tune it

What each term does

  • P pushes back in proportion to rate error. It sets the crossover frequency, which is how quickly the quad follows the sticks. Too much and the loop runs out of phase: it rings, then oscillates.
  • D pushes against rate change. It adds phase lead near crossover, which damps overshoot and lets you run more P. It also amplifies gyro noise and delay effects at high frequency, so too much D oscillates fast and heats motors.
  • I accumulates error over time. It cancels steady torques (off-centre battery, wind) that P alone leaves as a permanent error. Too much adds phase lag at low frequency: slow wobble, bounce-back after flips.
  • D filter smooths the D term. Lower cutoff means less noise but more lag.
  • Level gain Kₐ is the angle-mode outer loop. Keep its crossover at least 4× below the rate loop's.

A tuning recipe

  1. Start in Acro with I and D at zero. Raise P until a kick produces ringing, then back off by a third.
  2. Add D until the step-response overshoot is under about 20% and the Bode phase margin is above 45°.
  3. Set CG offset to 10 mm. Raise I until the roll angle no longer drifts, keeping phase margin above 45°.
  4. Check gain margin is above 6 dB and Ms below 1.6. Then add gyro noise and confirm the motor trace stays calm.
  5. Switch to Angle mode and raise Kₐ until the angle step has little overshoot.

Experiments

  • Load Example: hover at 5 m, then the P too high preset and press Kick roll. Slow to 0.25× and watch the motor arrows fight.
  • With Locked-in, raise loop delay to 12 ms. Watch the phase margin collapse and the Nyquist curve swing toward −1.
  • Pick No I term, set CG offset to 10 mm and hover. The quad leans and slides. Add I back and it recovers.
  • Pick D too high and raise gyro noise. The motor chart turns into a band of noise before it becomes unstable.
