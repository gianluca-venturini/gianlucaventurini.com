Scope & reference notes

A transparent reconstruction.

This is a self-contained, offline 3D HTML asset inspired by the sea-level Raptor 3. It represents the major full-flow staged-combustion systems and illustrative subcomponents, including internal machinery.

It is not complete SpaceX CAD or an exhaustive bill of materials. The sources below do not provide a complete Raptor 3 internal model. Dimensions, blade counts, injector counts, seals, valve arrangements, cooling paths, mounting details and internal routing here are conceptual. Individual selectability does not imply that a modeled object is a separate manufactured part; Raptor 3 integrates many functions.

Blue identifies oxygen-side hardware; gold identifies methane-side hardware. These are explanatory colors, not actual material finishes. The exploded view separates objects for inspection and does not represent an assembly procedure. Fluid particles are an X-ray overlay, visible through solid hardware. Their direction is illustrative; speed, density, colors and mixing are not a physical simulation. In exploded view, traces move with their individual components and separate at interfaces.

  • SpaceX updates — Raptor 3 integrated sensors, controllers and thermal protection.
  • SpaceX Starship — engine and vehicle context.
  • NASA: Full-flow staged combustion — the fuel-rich and oxygen-rich preburner/turbopump principle, not Raptor-specific geometry.

No network connection, external scripts or textures are needed. Use the system and individual checkboxes to select every modeled item. Hidden parts can always be selected from the component tree.
