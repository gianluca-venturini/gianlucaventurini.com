    Oil shockEnergy costs add 6 points to inflation, fading over about 9 months. Confidence dips slightly.JumpsRises a littleReal wages fall, rates riseA supply shock. Inflation and unemployment rise together, so the monetary loop hikes while the fiscal loop spends. The loops fight.
    Housing crashHouse prices drop 20% at once. New mortgages fall by about two thirds for 8 months, and construction demand collapses.FallsRisesMortgages, money supply, velocity fallLower rates help, but only after the 8-month freeze ends. Integral wind-up is a risk.
    Bank panic10% of the money supply vanishes as loans are called in. Mortgage lending freezes for 4 months and confidence drops.FallsRisesMoney supply drops, velocity risesMoney can't reach borrowers, so rate cuts transmit weakly at first.
    PandemicA 6-month lockdown caps employment at 88% of the workforce and confidence collapses. Unspent income piles up as forced savings. On reopening, supply chains clog (+4 points to inflation) while households spend those savings.Falls, then surgesSpikes to 12%+R&D stops, efficiency freezes, velocity collapses then recoversTwo phases with opposite needs. Rate cuts can't create jobs that are legally shut, so the integral winds up. On reopening, the stimulus meets pent-up demand and inflation overshoots, like 2021–22.
    WarThree years of defence spending at 5% of GDP, all borrowed, which hires workers directly.
